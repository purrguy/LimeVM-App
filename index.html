Why it's sour for reversers

🎲

Metamorphic VM

Opcode numbers, operand biases, handler spellings and dispatcher shape are re-rolled from your seed. Signature tools starve.

🔒

Encrypted everything

Strings, numbers and globals live as rotate+xor ciphertext, split into chunks. The decoder key stream never repeats.

🪤

Integrity tripwires

Every proto carries a checksum. Patch one byte and the program silently computes garbage instead of crashing loudly.

🤖

Anti-AI shield

Decoy banners plus encrypted dead protos. Scrapers and lifters trip over bait before they reach your logic.

Sour questions

  
Will my script still run exactly the same?
Yes. Every release passes 21 differential test cases (original vs obfuscated, byte-identical stdout) plus 140+ real-world scripts.

  
Can anyone reverse it?
Honest answer: a determined expert with dynamic analysis can eventually lift any client-side VM. LimeVM's job is making that cost weeks instead of minutes — fresh ISA per build, encrypted constants, tripwires.

  
How do accounts and activation work?
Make a free account here or in the app (username + password, Discord linking supported). Buy a tier on Discord and staff activate you — your app and site both unlock. License files still work for offline-first setups.

  
Does it work offline?
Yes. Licenses verify offline for 14 days between check-ins; obfuscation itself never needs the internet.

  
Is there really a free Discord obfuscator?
Yes — /obfuscate in the Discord server, once per day, up to 3000 chars. Same engine, nerfed demo preset.
